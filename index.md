# Privacy Policy for AuraFit

**Effective Date: May 4, 2026**

AuraFit ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the AuraFit mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information
When you create an account, we collect:
*   **Account Data**: Name, email address, and authentication credentials (via Firebase).
*   **Profile Data**: Height, weight, age, and gender.
*   **User-Generated Content**: Profile photos and fitness gallery images.

### 1.2 Health and Fitness Data
To provide our services, we process:
*   **Workout Logs**: Exercise names, sets, reps, weights, and muscle groups trained.
*   **Nutrition Logs**: Calorie and macronutrient intake (protein, carbs, fat).
*   **Personal Records**: Your best performance metrics for various exercises.
*   **Safety Readiness**: Self-reported data regarding physical readiness and injuries.

### 1.3 AI Interactions
*   **AuraFit AI**: We use Google Generative AI to provide fitness advice. Interactions with the AI are processed to generate responses but are governed by our data retention policies.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information to:
*   Track and visualize your fitness and nutrition progress.
*   Calculate calorie targets and training goals.
*   Provide personalized AI-driven fitness insights.
*   Maintain account security and verify subscriptions.
*   Send local notifications for workout reminders.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
*   **Cloud Storage**: Your data is securely stored using Firebase (Google Cloud).
*   **Local Storage**: Some data may be cached on your device for offline access.
*   **Security**: We implement industry-standard security measures provided by Firebase to protect your data.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services:
*   **Firebase (Google)**: For authentication, database storage, and file hosting.
*   **Google Generative AI (Gemini)**: To power the AuraFit AI assistant.
*   **Android/iOS Systems**: For local notifications and device-level permissions.

## 5. Your Rights
You have the right to:
*   **Access and Edit**: You can update your profile information within the App settings.
*   **Data Deletion**: You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us.
*   **Permissions**: You can revoke permissions (Camera, Storage, Audio) at any time via your device settings.

## 6. Children's Privacy
AuraFit is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date.
